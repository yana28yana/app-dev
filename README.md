# Series: Apothecary Diaries

## Synopsis
*A young woman named Maomao uses her extensive knowledge of medicine, herbs, and poisons to solve mysteries inside the palace*

## Main Characters
- Maomao
- Jinshi
- Gyokuyou
- Lihua
- Gaoshun

## Why I Love it 
It offers a different vibe of romance anime. Maomao's role as protagonist shows a refreshing character because she is independent, clever, curious, and skillful. Her chemistry with Jinshi adds humor and romantic tension which I love.

## Favorite Quote
> "Knowledge is more valuable than appearances."
